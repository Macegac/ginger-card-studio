Ginger Phone Probe

Checks the six browser capabilities the single-file Ginger port depends on. Run it on the phone you actually intend to use, over https — a file:// open will fail the service-worker check for reasons unrelated to your device.

Automatic checks

Round-trip with a real card

The decisive test. Pick a character card PNG — ideally one saved by Ginger, so it carries a compressed ginger chunk. This reads the real chunks, inflates the compressed one, decodes the card, and rebuilds the file.


  


  


  
Nothing leaves the device. The card you pick is read in memory, never uploaded.
